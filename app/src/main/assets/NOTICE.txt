Offline document OCR
================================================================================

PP-OCRv5 mobile models, PaddlePaddle; ONNX distribution by RapidAI/RapidOCR v3.9.2.

================================================================================
Offline speech recognition runtime and models
================================================================================

sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 (k2-fsa), Apache-2.0: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
Full license text: licenses/sherpa-onnx-Apache-2.0.txt. Used for on-device speech
recognition, voice activity detection and speaker embeddings (caption mode).
Silero VAD (snakers4/silero-vad), MIT: licenses/silero-vad-MIT.txt.

Models are downloaded on first use, or bundled in the offline edition only:
- Paraformer streaming zh-en (Alibaba DAMO / FunASR), Apache-2.0; sherpa-onnx export.
- SenseVoice Small (FunAudioLLM), released under the FunASR model license
  (attribution and model-name retention required); sherpa-onnx int8 export.
  https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/SenseVoice
- Zipformer streaming zh (k2-fsa), Apache-2.0.
- 3D-Speaker CAM++ speaker embedding zh-cn 16k-common (Alibaba 3D-Speaker), Apache-2.0;
  ONNX export published by sherpa-onnx. Used only to tell speakers apart in caption mode;
  voiceprints stay on the device.

English glide typing word-pair statistics
================================================================================

english_bigrams.txt and english_contractions.txt hold word and word-pair frequencies
derived (counts only, no text) by tools/glide_lab/build_english_bigrams.py from:
- Peter Norvig, Natural Language Corpus Data (count_1w.txt, count_2w.txt), MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2010-2017 Peter Norvig: https://github.com/norvig/pytudes
- Cornell Movie-Dialogs Corpus, Cristian Danescu-Niculescu-Mizil and Lillian Lee (2011):
  https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~cristian/Cornell_Movie-Dialogs_Corpus.html

================================================================================
Spanish word list
================================================================================

spanish/words.txt is the Spanish completion word list (one word per line, most frequent first),
built by tools/spanish/build_words.py:
- Word frequencies: Hermit Dave, FrequencyWords (content/2018/es/es_50k.txt, from OpenSubtitles 2018),
  commit 525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6. Code MIT; word lists CC-BY-SA-4.0.
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
- Spelling filter only (not shipped): LibreOffice Spanish spelling dictionary as packaged in
  the npm module dictionary-es 4.0.0 (GPL-3.0 OR LGPL-3.0 OR MPL-1.1), checked with spylls.
  https://github.com/wooorm/dictionaries

https://github.com/mozillazg/pinyin-data

listed in that repository.

================================================================================
Chinese association word-completion marks
================================================================================

association/lccc-base-v1.completions.gz marks which rows of the bundled association table
finish the last word instead of starting the next one (bits only, no word list). It is
generated by tools/ime_lab/ngram/tag_word_completions.py using the word list of
jieba 0.42.1 (jieba/dict.txt), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2013 Sun Junyi:
https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba
