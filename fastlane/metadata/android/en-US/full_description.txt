CyIME is an Android input method made by Cyletix, running on the Rime engine. The goal is the best open-source keyboard there is: dictionaries, handwriting, voice, photo text recognition and live captions all run on the phone, so everything works offline.

Highlights:
* Everything offline - the full package bundles voice, handwriting, prediction and the 9-key phrase model
* Its own 9-key model - type a whole sentence and pick words key by key
* Many ways to type - 26-key Pinyin, 9-key, 14-key; six Shuangpin layouts (Xiaohe, Shoudao, Ziranma and more) plus your own; Wubi, stroke, Cangjie, Zhuyin; Japanese, English and Spanish
* Handwriting - several Chinese, English or Japanese characters at once, math formulas as LaTeX, S Pen support
* Live captions for people who are hard of hearing - tells speakers apart, works during calls, vibrates when someone says your name
* Layout and looks - fixed, floating or split; color themes, glass effect, your own picture as background
* Clipboard - history, pinning, search, groups; SMS codes shown in the candidate bar
